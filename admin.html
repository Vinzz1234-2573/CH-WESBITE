Connect the database

This page needs your Supabase details before anyone can sign in.

  1. In Supabase, run supabase/children-home-setup.sql (SQL Editor → New query → paste → Run).
  2. Open supabase-config.js and paste your Project URL and publishable key.
  3. Upload the file to GitHub and reload this page.

The full steps are in ADMIN-GUIDE.md.

Form responses

The Google Sheets that collect your Google Form answers, in one place.

How to get a sheet link: open the Google Form → Responses tab → Link to Sheets (or View in Sheets) → copy the sheet’s address from the browser and paste it here with Edit.
Keep the sheets private. They hold people’s names and phone numbers. Leave each sheet’s sharing on Restricted (only your team), and never use Publish to the web. The preview below only appears when you’re signed in to Google in this browser with access to the sheet; otherwise use Open in Google Sheets.
